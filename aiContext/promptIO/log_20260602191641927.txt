MANUAL TEST CASE : TC-ProductPurchase-01
----------------------------
Scenario: Verify user is able to purchase a product successfully.

1. Launch https://www.demoblaze.com/index.html in chrome.
2. Click Login link in the nav bar.
3. Enter username: wwq221
4. Enter password: wwq221
5. After login, verify logged in user is displaying in the nav bar (Welcome wwq221).
6. Select Samsung galaxy s6.
7. Click Add to Cart button.
8. Verify "Product added." message in the pop up and click OK.
9. Click Cart link in the nav bar. Wait for selected product with Delete link is displayed.
10. Click on Place Order button.
11. Enter order details: name: wwq2221, Country: India, City: Hyderabad, Credit card: 12345, Month: 12, Year: 2027
12. Click on Purchase button.
13. Validate: "Thank you for your purchase!" text, order id generated, amount, card number, user name. Click Ok.
14. Wait for Logout link in the navbar.
15. Click Logout link.
